## Application Scope

### Investigation Center

The connected Investigation Center receives overview, queue, detail, timeline,
reconstruction, comparison, correction/retraction, relationship, evidence-
reference handoff, policy-preview, and investigation-health pages. These
surfaces consume generated adapters only and preserve loading, empty, partial,
stale, degraded, denied, conflict, failed, corrected, retracted, disputed,
unknown, unavailable, and recovery states.

Work views use server-shaped aggregates, allowlisted filters, stable ordering,
bounded cursor pagination, visible filter state, and authoritative list/detail
navigation. The client may not create an unbounded universal queue or join
investigation, evidence, review, relationship, and policy collections into a
new truth source.

### Timeline And Reconstruction

Record sequence remains the append-history authority. Event-time mode exposes
source, precision, range, clock condition, ties, and unknown state. The
semantic table/list is complete and authoritative; an optional visual lane is
limited to the returned page and cannot change order or meaning.

Reconstruction and comparison use exact revisions. Responses include digest,
profile, included and omitted components, completeness, limitations, and
later-change warnings. The browser cannot claim authoritative reconstruction
by replaying cached events or produce a generated historical narrative as
truth.

### Correction And Retraction

Original and successor records remain visible. Impact status is tracked per
accepted target as pending, applied, blocked, failed, or superseded. Missing or
incomplete impact closure remains prominent. No historical entry, review,
relationship, evidence reference, or policy preview is silently overwritten or
automatically reversed.

### Evidence Desk

Evidence Desk is independently authorized. It presents opaque reference
identity, availability, digest observation, provenance, custody, signature or
timestamp observation, access, and legal assessment as separate dimensions.
It never shows a single verified badge, evidence confidence score, confirmed
identity, guilt, authenticity, admissibility, or proof-of-event conclusion.

The provenance graph is bounded and read-only. Authoritative node, edge, and
custody tables provide complete semantic and keyboard workflows. Custody is
not inferred from provenance. Generated PROV output is explicitly lossy; import
and conformance claims stay disabled.

### Source And Policy Boundary

The UI displays source-not-resolved and media-not-rendered states. It cannot
resolve, copy, render, preview, stream, snapshot, download, print, export,
delete, modify, hold, or release source evidence. It receives no credential,
secret reference, operational locator, signed source URL, raw provider
response, filesystem path, object-store key, or hidden media endpoint.

Policy Preview Studio is generated-only and non-operative. It may show policy
references, target sets, conflicts, inclusion and exclusion reasons, simulated
outcomes, residuals, completeness, and limitations. It has no execute, approve,
sign, activate, release, deliver, download, print, delete, hold, retention-
change, disposition, or export control.
