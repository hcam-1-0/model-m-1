## Planned P5.4 Baseline

- Command Center remains the primary dashboard.
- Intelligence Center is a connected specialist portal with grouped pages.
- Observation, inference, hypothesis, candidate, proposed alert, review,
  correction, and lifecycle are distinct semantic types.
- Graph and spatial views are bounded enhancements with authoritative tables.
- Rule explanations are exact revisioned traces, not prose-only summaries.
- Candidate comparisons expose field-level uncertainty, contradiction, and
  abstention; identity is not established.
- Review policy and quorum remain server authoritative.
- Consequential mutations require strong ETag, expected revision, idempotency,
  explicit confirmation, immutable receipt, and authoritative refetch.
- Conflicts stop automatic submission and require deliberate reconsideration.
- Events invalidate; HTTP confirms current state and authority.
- Corrections are append-only, visible across impacted views, and lock stale
  mutation until refetch.
- Resource profiles change rendering capacity only, never authority or safety.
- No review can trigger notification, dispatch, enforcement, or external action.
