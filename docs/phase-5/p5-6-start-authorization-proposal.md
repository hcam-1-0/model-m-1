Security, And Operations. It translates the accepted R1 architecture into an
exact local generated-only delivery scope. Preparing this package does not

## Admin Center Scope

user, membership, session, and access-review; role, permission, capability, and
SoD; policy and change-request; camera and stream governance; integration,
provider, and opaque secret-reference; feature and configuration; resource,

subject, revision, ETag, before/after comparison, validation, dependencies,

receipt states. The generated flow terminates in a non-effective receipt or an

Restore, Direct Control, and equivalent controls are absent.
