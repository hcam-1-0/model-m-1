# H-CAM Collaboration Code

H-CAM contributors are expected to collaborate professionally, protect sensitive systems and data, and make technical decisions through evidence and review.

## Expected Conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions and document important tradeoffs.
- Respect approval, ownership, security, and data-handling boundaries.
- Use synthetic or redacted examples and stop work that risks unauthorized disclosure.
- Welcome good-faith questions and help contributors understand the system and its constraints.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, credential sharing, unauthorized camera access, publication of sensitive data, and attempts to bypass review or safety controls are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or data-safety concerns privately to repository administrators through GitHub. Administrators may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or revoke access when necessary to protect contributors, systems, or data. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and applied consistently.
