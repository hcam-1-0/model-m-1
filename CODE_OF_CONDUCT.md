# Collaborator Code Of Conduct

## Expected Conduct

H-CAM collaborators communicate respectfully, review technical claims on their
evidence, give actionable feedback, protect sensitive information, credit
other contributors, and keep disagreements focused on engineering decisions.

Contributors are expected to:

- document assumptions, limitations, risks, and uncertainty;
- separate observations, inferences, hypotheses, and confirmed facts;
- avoid overstating model, identity, security, performance, or deployment
  capabilities;
- preserve accessibility, privacy, authorization, audit, and human-review
  requirements;
- never pressure another contributor to disclose credentials, personal data,
  private media, Government information, or security-sensitive details;
- stop and escalate privately when safety or authorization is unclear.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate
misrepresentation, unauthorized access, retaliation, and disclosure of private
or security-sensitive material are not acceptable.

## Reporting Conduct Concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the `hcam-1-0` organization owners using
an established private team channel. Do not publish sensitive personal details
or security evidence in an issue or discussion. Organization owners may limit
access or participation when needed to protect collaborators and project data.

This code applies to organization repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, Project items, and project-related team communication.
